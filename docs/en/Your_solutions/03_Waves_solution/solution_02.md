# 2. String Harmonics

## 📘 Key Definitions and Formulas

### 1. Standing Waves on a String
For a string fixed at both ends, the fundamental mode $n=1$ has:
- **One antinode**
- **Two nodes at the ends**

---

### 2. Relationship Between Length and Wavelength

For the fundamental frequency:

$$L=\frac{\lambda}{2}$$

So:

$$\lambda=2L$$

---

### 3. Wave Speed Formula

The general wave relation:

$$v=f\lambda$$

Where:
- $v$ — wave speed $m/s$
- $f$ — frequency $Hz$
- $\lambda$ — wavelength $m$

---

## 🧮 Step-by-Step Solution

### 🔹 Given:
- Length of string: $L=64\,\text{cm}=0.64\,\text{m}$
- Fundamental frequency: $f=330\,\text{Hz}$

---

## 🎸 Step 1: Find the Wavelength

Using:

$$\lambda=2L$$

Substitute:

$$\lambda=2\times0.64$$

$$\lambda=1.28\,\text{m}$$

---

## ⚡ Step 2: Find the Wave Speed

Using:

$$v=f\lambda$$

Substitute:

$$v=330\times1.28$$

$$v=422.4\,\text{m/s}$$

---

## 🎯 Final Answer

$$v\approx422.4\,\text{m/s}$$

---

## 💡 Final Insight

✔️ The wave speed depends on the **string properties** tension and linear density, not directly on length or frequency.  
✔️ The length helps determine the wavelength in standing wave conditions.
