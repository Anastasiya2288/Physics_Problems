# 5. Echo Ranging

## 📘 Key Definitions and Formulas

### 1. Wave Speed Formula

The general relation between distance, speed, and time:

$$v=\frac{d}{t}$$

Rearranged to find distance:

$$d=vt$$

---

### 2. Important Concept: Echo

When dealing with echoes:
- The sound travels **to the cliff and back**
- The measured time is for a **round trip**

So the total distance is:

$$d_{total}=2d$$

Thus:

$$2d=vt$$

And solving for the one-way distance:

$$d=\frac{vt}{2}$$

---

## 🧮 Step-by-Step Solution

### 🔹 Given:
- Time: $t=1\,\text{s}$
- Speed of sound: $v=343\,\text{m/s}$

---

## 🔹 Step 1: Use Echo Formula

$$d=\frac{vt}{2}$$

---

## 🔹 Step 2: Substitute Values

$$d=\frac{343\times1}{2}$$

---

## 🔹 Step 3: Calculate

$$d=171.5\,\text{m}$$

---

## 🎯 Final Answer

$$d=171.5\,\text{m}$$

---

## 💡 Final Insight

✔️ The division by 2 is crucial because the measured time includes both forward and return travel.  
✔️ Without dividing by 2, the distance would be overestimated.
