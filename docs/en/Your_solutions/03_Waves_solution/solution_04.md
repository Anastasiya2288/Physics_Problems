# 4. Phase Difference

## 📘 Key Definitions and Formulas

### 1. Wave Phase
A wave can be written as:

$$y(x,t)=A\sin(kx-\omega t)$$

- The expression inside the sine $kx-\omega t$ is called the **phase**.

---

### 2. Wave Number

The wave number $k$ is defined as:

$$k=\frac{2\pi}{\lambda}$$

---

### 3. Phase Difference Formula

The phase difference between two points separated by distance $\Delta x$ is:

$$\Delta\phi=k\Delta x$$

---

## 🧮 Step-by-Step Solution

### 🔹 Given:
- Distance between points: $\Delta x=\frac{\lambda}{3}$

---

## 🔹 Step 1: Use Phase Difference Formula

$$\Delta\phi=k\Delta x$$

---

## 🔹 Step 2: Substitute $k=\frac{2\pi}{\lambda}$

$$\Delta\phi=\frac{2\pi}{\lambda}\cdot\frac{\lambda}{3}$$

---

## 🔹 Step 3: Simplify

Cancel $\lambda$:

$$\Delta\phi=\frac{2\pi}{3}$$

---

## 🎯 Final Answer

$$\Delta\phi=\frac{2\pi}{3}\ \text{radians}$$

---

## 💡 Final Insight

✔️ A full wavelength corresponds to a phase change of $2\pi$.  
✔️ Therefore, $\lambda/3$ corresponds to $\frac{1}{3}$ of a full cycle → $\frac{2\pi}{3}$ radians.
