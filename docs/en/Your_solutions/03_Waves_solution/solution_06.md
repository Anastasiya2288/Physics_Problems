# 6. Wave Equation

## 📘 Key Definitions and Formulas

### 1. General Form of a Traveling Wave

$$y(x,t)=A\sin(kx-\omega t)$$

Where:
- $A$ — amplitude (maximum displacement)
- $k$ — wave number
- $\omega$ — angular frequency

---

### 2. Wave Number

$$k=\frac{2\pi}{\lambda}$$

So:

$$\lambda=\frac{2\pi}{k}$$

---

### 3. Angular Frequency

$$\omega=2\pi f$$

So:

$$f=\frac{\omega}{2\pi}$$

---

### 4. Wave Speed

$$v=\frac{\omega}{k}$$

---

## 🧮 Step-by-Step Solution

### 🔹 Given Wave Equation:

$$y(x,t)=0.05\sin(2\pi x-50\pi t)$$

---

## 🔹 Step 1: Identify Parameters

Compare with:

$$y(x,t)=A\sin(kx-\omega t)$$

We get:

- $A=0.05$
- $k=2\pi$
- $\omega=50\pi$

---

## 🎯 a) Amplitude

$$A=0.05\,\text{m}$$

---

## 🔹 Step 2: Find Wavelength

Using:

$$\lambda=\frac{2\pi}{k}$$

Substitute:

$$\lambda=\frac{2\pi}{2\pi}$$

$$\lambda=1\,\text{m}$$

---

## 🎯 b) Wavelength

$$\lambda=1\,\text{m}$$

---

## 🔹 Step 3: Find Frequency

Using:

$$f=\frac{\omega}{2\pi}$$

Substitute:

$$f=\frac{50\pi}{2\pi}$$

$$f=25\,\text{Hz}$$

---

## 🎯 c) Frequency

$$f=25\,\text{Hz}$$

---

## 🔹 Step 4: Find Wave Speed

Using:

$$v=\frac{\omega}{k}$$

Substitute:

$$v=\frac{50\pi}{2\pi}$$

$$v=25\,\text{m/s}$$

---

## 🎯 d) Wave Speed

$$v=25\,\text{m/s}$$

---

## 💡 Final Summary

- Amplitude: $$A=0.05\,\text{m}$$  
- Wavelength: $$\lambda=1\,\text{m}$$  
- Frequency: $$f=25\,\text{Hz}$$  
- Wave speed: $$v=25\,\text{m/s}$$

✔️ All parameters are directly extracted by comparing with the standard wave equation.
