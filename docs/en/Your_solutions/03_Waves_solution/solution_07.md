# 7. Standing Wave Modes

## 📘 Key Definitions and Formulas

### 1. Standing Waves on a String

For a string fixed at both ends:
- Nodes are at the ends
- Antinodes are points of maximum vibration

---

### 2. Relationship Between Length and Wavelength

For the $n$-th harmonic:

$$L=\frac{n\lambda}{2}$$

Where:
- $L$ — length of the string
- $\lambda$ — wavelength
- $n$ — number of antinodes (harmonic number)

---

### 3. Rearranged Formula

To find wavelength:

$$\lambda=\frac{2L}{n}$$

---

## 🧮 Step-by-Step Solution

### 🔹 Given:
- Length: $L=80\,\text{cm}=0.80\,\text{m}$
- Number of antinodes: $n=4$

---

## 🔹 Step 1: Use Formula

$$\lambda=\frac{2L}{n}$$

---

## 🔹 Step 2: Substitute Values

$$\lambda=\frac{2\times0.80}{4}$$

---

## 🔹 Step 3: Calculate

$$\lambda=\frac{1.60}{4}=0.40\,\text{m}$$

---

## 🎯 Final Answer

$$\lambda=0.40\,\text{m}$$

---

## 💡 Final Insight

✔️ Each antinode corresponds to one half-wavelength segment.  
✔️ More antinodes → shorter wavelength for the same string length.
