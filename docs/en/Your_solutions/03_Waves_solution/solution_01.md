# 1. Wave Properties

## 📘 Key Definitions and Formulas

### 1. Wave Speed Relation
The fundamental relationship between wave speed, frequency, and wavelength is:

$$v=f\lambda$$

Where:
- $v$ — wave speed (m/s)
- $f$ — frequency (Hz)
- $\lambda$ — wavelength (m)

---

### 2. Rearranged Formula for Wavelength
To find wavelength, we solve for $\lambda$:

$$\lambda=\frac{v}{f}$$

---

### 3. Important Concept
- **Frequency ($f$)** does **not change** when a wave moves from one medium to another.
- **Wave speed ($v$)** changes depending on the medium.
- Therefore, **wavelength ($\lambda$)** changes accordingly.

---

## 🧮 Step-by-Step Solution

### 🔹 Given:
- Frequency: $f=440\,\text{Hz}$
- Speed of sound in air: $v_{air}=343\,\text{m/s}$
- Speed of sound in water: $v_{water}=1482\,\text{m/s}$

---

## 🌬️ 1. Wavelength in Air

Using the formula:

$$\lambda_{air}=\frac{v_{air}}{f}$$

Substitute values:

$$\lambda_{air}=\frac{343}{440}$$

$$\lambda_{air}\approx0.7795\,\text{m}$$

### ✅ Final Answer:
$$\lambda_{air}\approx0.78\,\text{m}$$

---

## 🌊 2. Wavelength in Water

Using the same formula:

$$\lambda_{water}=\frac{v_{water}}{f}$$

Substitute values:

$$\lambda_{water}=\frac{1482}{440}$$

$$\lambda_{water}\approx3.368\,\text{m}$$

### ✅ Final Answer:
$$\lambda_{water}\approx3.37\,\text{m}$$

---

## 🎯 Final Summary

- Wavelength in air: $$\lambda_{air}\approx0.78\,\text{m}$$
- Wavelength in water: $$\lambda_{water}\approx3.37\,\text{m}$$

✔️ The wavelength increases in water because the speed of sound is much higher, while frequency remains constant.
