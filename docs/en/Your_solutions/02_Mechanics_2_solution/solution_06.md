# 📘 Energy Dissipation — Bouncing Ball

---

## 🔑 Key Definitions and Formulas

### 📌 Mechanical Energy

At height $h$:

$$
E=mgh
$$

---

### 📌 Energy Loss per Bounce

If the ball loses 30% of its energy, it retains:

$$
E_{\text{after}}=0.7E_{\text{before}}
$$

---

### 📌 Relationship Between Energy and Height

Since:

$$
E=mgh
$$

and $m$ and $g$ are constant:

$$
E\propto h
$$

So:

$$
h_{\text{after}}=0.7h_{\text{before}}
$$

---

## 🧩 Given:

- Initial height: $h_0=2.0\ \text{m}$
- Energy retained after each bounce: $70\% = 0.7$

---

# 🔍 Step 1: Height after First Bounce

$$
h_1=0.7\cdot h_0
$$

$$
h_1=0.7\cdot2.0=1.4\ \text{m}
$$

---

# 🔍 Step 2: Height after Second Bounce

$$
h_2=0.7\cdot h_1
$$

$$
h_2=0.7\cdot1.4
$$

$$
h_2=0.98\ \text{m}
$$

---

## ✅ Final Answer:

$$
h_2\approx0.98\ \text{m}
$$

---

# 🎯 Conclusion

- Each bounce reduces height by the same factor (0.7)
- This creates a **geometric decay** of height:
  
$$
h_n=0.7^n\cdot h_0
$$
