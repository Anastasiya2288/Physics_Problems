# 📘 Inelastic Collision — Runner and Cart

---

## 🔑 Key Definitions and Formulas

### 📌 Conservation of Momentum

In any collision (if no external forces act):

$$
m_1v_1+m_2v_2=\left(m_1+m_2\right)v_f
$$

---

### 📌 Inelastic Collision

- Objects **stick together after collision**
- Momentum is conserved
- Kinetic energy is **not conserved**

---

### 📌 Kinetic Energy

$$
K=\frac{1}{2}mv^2
$$

---

## 🧩 Given:

- $m_1=70\ \text{kg}$ (runner)
- $v_1=3\ \text{m/s}$
- $m_2=140\ \text{kg}$ (cart)
- $v_2=0$

---

# 🔍 Step 1: Apply Conservation of Momentum

$$
m_1v_1+m_2v_2=\left(m_1+m_2\right)v_f
$$

---

### 🔄 Substitute values:

$$
70\cdot3+140\cdot0=\left(70+140\right)v_f
$$

$$
210=210\cdot v_f
$$

---

# 🔍 Step 2: Solve for Final Velocity

$$
v_f=\frac{210}{210}=1\ \text{m/s}
$$

---

## ✅ Final Speed:

$$
v_f=1\ \text{m/s}
$$

---

# 🔍 Step 3: Check Kinetic Energy Conservation

### 🔹 Initial Kinetic Energy:

$$
K_i=\frac{1}{2}\cdot70\cdot3^2
$$

$$
K_i=35\cdot9=315\ \text{J}
$$

---

### 🔹 Final Kinetic Energy:

$$
K_f=\frac{1}{2}\cdot210\cdot1^2
$$

$$
K_f=105\ \text{J}
$$

---

# 🔍 Step 4: Compare Energies

$$
K_i=315\ \text{J},\quad K_f=105\ \text{J}
$$

---

## ❗ Conclusion About Energy

- Kinetic energy **decreased**
- Therefore, kinetic energy is **not conserved**

---

## 🧠 Explanation

In an **inelastic collision**:
- Some kinetic energy is transformed into:
  - heat
  - sound
  - internal deformation

However:
- **Momentum is always conserved**
- **Energy is conserved overall**, but not as kinetic energy

---

# 🎯 Final Results

- Final velocity:  
  $$v_f=1\ \text{m/s}$$

- Kinetic energy:  
  ❌ Not conserved
