# 📘 Dynamics with Friction — Two-Block System

---

## 🔑 Key Definitions and Formulas

### 📌 Newton’s Second Law

$$
F_{\text{net}}=ma
$$

---

### 📌 Kinetic Friction Force

$$
f_k=\mu_k N
$$

where:
- $\mu_k$ — coefficient of kinetic friction
- $N$ — normal force

---

### 📌 Important Idea

There are **two friction forces acting on the 10 kg block**:

1. Friction with the ground  
2. Friction with the 5 kg block (which is tied and does not move)

Both oppose motion.

---

## 🧩 Given:

- $m_1=5\ \text{kg}$ (top block, fixed)
- $m_2=10\ \text{kg}$ (bottom block)
- $F=45\ \text{N}$
- $\mu_k=0.2$
- $g=9.81\ \text{m/s}^2$

---

# 🔍 Step 1: Friction Between 5 kg and 10 kg Block

Normal force between them:

$$
N_1=m_1g=5\cdot9.81=49.05\ \text{N}
$$

Friction force:

$$
f_1=\mu_k N_1=0.2\cdot49.05=9.81\ \text{N}
$$

---

# 🔍 Step 2: Friction Between 10 kg Block and Ground

Normal force includes both masses:

$$
N_2=\left(m_1+m_2\right)g=15\cdot9.81=147.15\ \text{N}
$$

Friction force:

$$
f_2=0.2\cdot147.15=29.43\ \text{N}
$$

---

# 🔍 Step 3: Total Opposing Force

$$
f_{\text{total}}=f_1+f_2=9.81+29.43=39.24\ \text{N}
$$

---

# 🔍 Step 4: Net Force on 10 kg Block

$$
F_{\text{net}}=45-39.24=5.76\ \text{N}
$$

---

# 🔍 Step 5: Acceleration

$$
a=\frac{F_{\text{net}}}{m_2}
$$

$$
a=\frac{5.76}{10}
$$

$$
a=0.576\ \text{m/s}^2
$$

---

## ✅ Final Answer:

$$
a\approx0.58\ \text{m/s}^2
$$

---

# 🎯 Conclusion

- The bottom block is slowed by **two friction forces**
- Even though the applied force is large, friction significantly reduces acceleration
- The top block being fixed increases resistance
