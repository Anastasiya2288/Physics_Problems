
# Gauss's Law — Electric Flux Through a Spherical Surface

## Useful Definitions and Formulas

### 1. Electric Flux

Electric flux describes how much electric field passes through a surface.

The mathematical definition is:

$$
\Phi_E=\oint \vec{E}\cdot d\vec{A}
$$

where:

- $\Phi_E$ — electric flux
- $\vec{E}$ — electric field vector
- $d\vec{A}$ — small area vector on the surface
- $\oint$ — integral over a closed surface

---

### 2. Gauss's Law

Gauss's Law states that the total electric flux through any closed surface depends only on the charge enclosed inside the surface.

The formula is:

\Phi_E=\frac{Q_{\mathrm{enc}}}{\varepsilon_0}

where:

- $\Phi_E$ — total electric flux
- $Q_{\mathrm{enc}}$ — enclosed charge
- $\varepsilon_0$ — permittivity of free space

The value of the permittivity of free space is:

$$
\varepsilon_0=8.85\times10^{-12}\ \mathrm{C^2/(N\cdot m^2)}
$$

---

### 3. Important Concept

For a spherical surface centered around a point charge:

- the electric field is symmetric,
- the electric field lines pass perpendicularly through the sphere,
- therefore Gauss's Law can be applied directly.

The radius of the sphere does NOT affect the total flux as long as the charge remains inside the surface.

---

# Step-by-Step Solution

## Step 1. Write the Given Information

Point charge:

$$
Q=+2\ \mathrm{C}
$$

Radius of sphere:

$$
r=1\ \mathrm{m}
$$

Permittivity of free space:

$$
\varepsilon_0=8.85\times10^{-12}\ \mathrm{C^2/(N\cdot m^2)}
$$

---

## Step 2. Apply Gauss's Law

Using Gauss's Law:

$$
\Phi_E=\frac{Q}{\varepsilon_0}
$$

Substitute the known values:

$$
\Phi_E=\frac{2}{8.85\times10^{-12}}
$$

---

## Step 3. Perform the Calculation

Calculate the division:

$$
\Phi_E\approx2.26\times10^{11}\ \mathrm{N\cdot m^2/C}
$$

---

# Final Answer

$$
\boxed{
\Phi_E\approx2.26\times10^{11}\ \mathrm{N\cdot m^2/C}
}
$$

---

# Explanation of the Result

- The electric flux is positive because the charge is positive.
- A positive charge produces electric field lines pointing outward.
- All electric field lines pass through the spherical surface.
- The flux depends only on the enclosed charge, not on the radius of the sphere.
