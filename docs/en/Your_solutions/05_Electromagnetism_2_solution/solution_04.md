# Magnetic Torque on a Current Loop

## Step-by-Step Solution

### Step 1: Convert dimensions to SI units

- Length = $0.10m$
- Width = $0.05m$

---

### Step 2: Calculate the area

$$
A=0.10\times0.05=0.005m^2
$$

---

### Step 3: Magnetic dipole moment

Given:
- $I=2A$

$$
\mu=I\times A=2\times0.005=0.01A\cdot m^2
$$

---

### Step 4: Calculate the torque

Given:
- $B=0.3T$
- $\sin(90^\circ)=1$

$$
\tau=\mu\times B\times\sin(\theta)
$$

$$
\tau=0.01\times0.3\times1=0.003Nm
$$

---

## Final Answer

$$
\tau=3\times10^{-3}Nm
$$

---

## Interpretation

The torque is maximal because the magnetic field is parallel to the loop's plane, meaning the loop experiences the strongest possible rotational effect.
