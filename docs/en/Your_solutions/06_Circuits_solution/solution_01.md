Given:
R₁ = 15 Ω
R₂ = 30 Ω
R₃ = 50 Ω
V = 12 V

-----------------------------------
1. SERIES CONNECTION
-----------------------------------

Equivalent resistance:

Req = R₁ + R₂ + R₃

Req = 15 + 30 + 50

Req = 95 Ω

Current from the battery:

I = V / Req

I = 12 / 95

I ≈ 0.126 A

Results (Series):
Req = 95 Ω
I ≈ 0.126 A


-----------------------------------
2. PARALLEL CONNECTION
-----------------------------------

Equivalent resistance:

1/Req = 1/R₁ + 1/R₂ + 1/R₃

1/Req = 1/15 + 1/30 + 1/50

1/Req = 0.0667 + 0.0333 + 0.0200

1/Req = 0.1200

Req = 1 / 0.1200

Req ≈ 8.33 Ω

Current from the battery:

I = V / Req

I = 12 / 8.33

I ≈ 1.44 A

Results (Parallel):
Req ≈ 8.33 Ω
I ≈ 1.44 A
