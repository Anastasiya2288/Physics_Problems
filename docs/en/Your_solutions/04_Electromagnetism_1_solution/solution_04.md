# 4. Force Comparison — Detailed Solution

## 🔹 Key Definitions and Formulas

### 1. Coulomb’s Law (Electric Force)

$$
F_e = k \frac{|q_e q_p|}{r^2}
$$

Where:
- $F_e$ — electric force  
- $k$  — Coulomb constant
- $q_e, q_p$ — charges of electron and proton  
- $r$ — distance between them  

---

### 2. Newton’s Law of Gravitation

$$
F_g = G \frac{m_e m_p}{r^2}
$$

Where:
- $F_g$ — gravitational force  
- $G$ = 6.67 \\times 10^{-11} \\text{N·m}^2/\text{kg}^2$  
- $m_e, m_p$ — masses of electron and proton  

---

### 3. Ratio of Forces

$$
\frac{F_e}{F_g} = \frac{k |q_e q_p|}{G m_e m_p}
$$

👉 Notice: $r^2$ cancels out!

---

## 🔹 Step-by-Step Solution

### Step 1: Known Constants

- $q_e = 1.6 \times 10^{-19}$ C  
- $q_p = 1.6 \times 10^{-19}$ C  
- $m_e = 9.11 \times 10^{-31}$ kg  
- $m_p = 1.67 \times 10^{-27}$ kg  
- $r = 5.3 \times 10^{-11}$ m  

---

## 🔹 Part 1: Electric Force

$$
F_e = k \frac{q_e q_p}{r^2}
$$

Substitute:

$$
F_e = 9 \times 10^9 \cdot \frac{(1.6 \times 10^{-19})^2}{(5.3 \times 10^{-11})^2}
$$

---

### Step 2: Simplify

$$
(1.6 \times 10^{-19})^2 = 2.56 \times 10^{-38}
$$

$$
(5.3 \times 10^{-11})^2 \approx 2.81 \times 10^{-21}
$$

$$
F_e = 9 \times 10^9 \cdot \frac{2.56 \times 10^{-38}}{2.81 \times 10^{-21}}
$$

$$
F_e \approx 9 \times 10^9 \cdot 9.11 \times 10^{-18}
$$

$$
F_e \approx 8.2 \times 10^{-8} \ \text{N}
$$

---

## 🔹 Part 2: Gravitational Force

$$
F_g = G \frac{m_e m_p}{r^2}
$$

Substitute:

$$
F_g = 6.67 \times 10^{-11} \cdot \frac{(9.11 \times 10^{-31})(1.67 \times 10^{-27})}{2.81 \times 10^{-21}}
$$

---

### Step 3: Simplify

$$
m_e m_p \approx 1.52 \times 10^{-57}
$$

$$
F_g = 6.67 \times 10^{-11} \cdot \frac{1.52 \times 10^{-57}}{2.81 \times 10^{-21}}
$$

$$
F_g \approx 6.67 \times 10^{-11} \cdot 5.41 \times 10^{-37}
$$

$$
F_g \approx 3.6 \times 10^{-47} \ \text{N}
$$

---

## 🔹 Part 3: Ratio of Forces

$$
\frac{F_e}{F_g} = \frac{8.2 \times 10^{-8}}{3.6 \times 10^{-47}}
$$

$$
\frac{F_e}{F_g} \approx 2.3 \times 10^{39}
$$

---

## 🔹 Final Answer

- Electric force:

$$
F_e \approx 8.2 \times 10^{-8} \ \text{N}
$$

- Gravitational force:

$$
F_g \approx 3.6 \times 10^{-47} \ \text{N}
$$

- Ratio:

$$
\frac{F_e}{F_g} \approx 2.3 \times 10^{39}
$$

---

## 🔹 Key Insight

- Electric force is **~10^{39} times stronger** than gravity at atomic scale  

👉 This is why:
- Atoms are held together by **electromagnetism**, not gravity  
- Gravity is completely negligible in atomic physics
