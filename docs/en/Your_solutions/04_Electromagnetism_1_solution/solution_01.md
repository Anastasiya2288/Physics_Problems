# 1. Coulomb’s Law — Detailed Solution

## 🔹 Key Definitions and Formulas

### 1. Coulomb’s Law

$$
F = k \frac{|q_1 q_2|}{r^2}
$$

Where:
- $F$ — magnitude of the electric force  
- $k$ = 9 \times 10^9 \ \text{N·m}^2/\text{C}^2$— Coulomb constant  
- $q_1, q_2$ — charges  
- $r$ — distance between charges  

---

### 2. Principle of Superposition

$$
\vec{F}_{\text{total}} = \sum \vec{F}_i
$$

---

### 3. Geometry of the Square

$$
r = \frac{a}{\sqrt{2}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}
$$

---

## 🔹 Step-by-Step Solution

### Step 1: System Description

- Four charges: $+1\ \text{C}$ at corners  
- One charge: $-2\ \text{C}$ at center  
- We need the **net force on the central charge**

---

### Step 2: Symmetry Insight

All four charges:
- Are identical  
- Are equally distant from the center  
- Pull the negative charge toward themselves  

So:
- All forces have equal magnitude  
- Directions are symmetric  

---

### Step 3: Force from One Charge

Distance:

$$
r = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}
$$

Apply Coulomb’s Law:

$$
F = k \frac{|q_1 q_2|}{r^2}
$$

Substitute:

$$
F = 9 \times 10^9 \cdot \frac{(1)(2)}{\left(\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\right)^2}
$$

Simplify:

$$
\left(\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\right)^2 = \frac{1}{2}
$$

$$
F = 9 \times 10^9 \cdot \frac{2}{1/2}
$$

$$
F = 9 \times 10^9 \cdot 4
$$

$$
F = 3.6 \times 10^{10} \ \text{N}
$$

---

### Step 4: Vector Components

Each force is diagonal → split into $x$ and $y$.

Because of symmetry:
- Left cancels right  
- Top cancels bottom  

---

### Step 5: Net Force

$$
\vec{F}_{\text{total}} = 0
$$

---

## 🔹 Final Answer

- Magnitude:
$$
F = 0 \ \text{N}
$$

- Direction: none (force is zero)

---

## 🔹 Key Insight

Even though each individual force is extremely large, perfect symmetry causes **complete cancellation**.

This is a classic physics result:
> Symmetry can eliminate even very large forces completely.
